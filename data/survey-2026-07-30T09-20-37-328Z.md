## Method and limits

- Sample frame: open issues discoverable through these queries:
  - `label:bounty is:issue state:open`
  - `label:"💎 Bounty" is:issue state:open`
  - `label:"bounty" is:issue state:open sort:updated`
  - `bounty in:title is:issue state:open`
  - `label:"help wanted" bounty in:body is:issue state:open`
- This is a sample of *label-discoverable* bounty issues, not a census of paid open-source work. Programs that do not label issues, and invite-only programs, are outside the frame.
- `LIKELY LEGIT` means provenance and payout evidence held up under the checks in `src/signals.js`. It is not a guarantee of payment.
- `UNVERIFIED` is the fail-closed default: it includes both thin-evidence targets and scans where the API withheld data.
- Reproduce with `node scripts/survey.js --limit 200`.
